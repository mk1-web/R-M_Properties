The UAE advantage

Why investin the UAE?

Invest where your wealth grows and your family thrives. The UAE has become one of the world’s top destinations for investors, entrepreneurs and families. It combines tax-free living, world-class safety and access to global markets. Whether you’re buying your first property or expanding your portfolio, the UAE gives you the stability, returns and lifestyle to build a lasting future.

0% personal income tax

There’s no tax on your salary, capital gains, inheritance or dividends, so you keep more of what you earn.

Low corporate tax

The rate is just 9%, and the first AED 375,000 of profit is tax-free. Qualifying Free Zone companies can pay 0%.

10-year Golden Visa

Own property worth AED 2 million or more and get long-term residency for you and your family. Off-plan and mortgaged properties can also qualify.

World’s safest city

Abu Dhabi has been ranked #1 in the world for safety for 10 years in a row, giving you real peace of mind.

Stable USD-pegged currency

The dirham is fixed to the US dollar, protecting your investment from currency swings.

100% ownership

You can own your business or property outright in designated areas and move your profits abroad freely.

Strong legal protection

The DIFC and ADGM courts operate under English Common Law, giving international investors a system they know and trust.

Global gateway

Two-thirds of the world’s population is within an 8-hour flight, supported by world-class ports, airports and Etihad Rail.

General information only, current at the time of writing and subject to change. It is not financial, tax, legal or immigration advice. Golden Visa eligibility is decided by the UAE authorities. Please take independent professional advice for your circumstances.
